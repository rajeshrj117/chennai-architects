05 — Our Design Process

Clear steps. No surprises.

STEP 01

Initial Consultation

A relaxed discussion — on call, at the studio or on WhatsApp. We understand your plot / flat, family needs, lifestyle and budget comfort.

STEP 02

Site Understanding & Requirements

Site visit & measurement across Chennai — light, breeze, Vastu inputs, apartment association rules and a detailed requirement brief.

STEP 03

Concept Planning

Floor plans, furniture layouts and elevation directions with mood boards — revised with you until the plan feels right.

STEP 04

3D Design & Material Selection

Photoreal 3D views, real material samples, lighting & sanitary selections — so cost and look are locked before execution.

STEP 05

Final Design / Execution Coordination

Working drawings, BOQ support and stage-wise coordination with your contractor or our execution partners.
